# Audio credits

Every file here is CC0 / public domain, or CC-BY with the attribution below. Each was trimmed, converted to
mono or stereo, level-matched and encoded as OGG Vorbis (about 96 kbps); none was otherwise re-mixed except where noted.
The jump whoosh is synthesised in `src/tv/audio.js` (Web Audio), so it has no file. The level themes (`THEME_SOUNDS` in
`src/tv/audio.js`) also reuse `ambience_wind.ogg`, `ambience_water.ogg` and the eagle cries with extra filtering and
lower levels (the night wind, the sunset breeze and distant waterfall, the far eagle), which need no extra files.

## Provenance notes

- `scream.ogg` and `creak.ogg` come from OpenGameArt re-uploads of Freesound recordings. The CC0 statements are the
  ones on the OpenGameArt pages / in the pack's text file; the original Freesound pages were not consulted (that site
  needs a login). Replace these two if a stricter chain of title is needed.
- The Wilhelm scream was not used. The other two voices in the same OpenGameArt pack (`aargh0`-`aargh2`, CC BY 3.0) were not used either.
- `ambience_crickets.ogg` comes from an OpenGameArt upload; the CC0 statement is the one on that page, and the recording's own
  origin was not verified. Replace it if a stricter chain of title is needed. A second file from the same site
  (`https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/crickets.mp3`, a different recording) was not used.
- Wikimedia Commons licences were read from each file page through the API (`extmetadata`) when the files were chosen.
  Public-domain recordings from PDSounds ("Gentle breeze and birds singing", "Shallow small river with stony riverbed") and the
  British Library "Royal Natal National Park dawn chorus" were evaluated and not used.
